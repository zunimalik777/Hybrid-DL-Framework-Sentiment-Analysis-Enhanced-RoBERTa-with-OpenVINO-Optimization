A Novel Hybrid Deep Learning Framework for Sentiment Analysis Based on Enhanced RoBERTa with OpenVINO Optimization
The fast development of digital technology and its accessibility have greatly raised online engagement, making internet-based interaction a crucial factor in daily life. This has led to the internet becoming an important part of people's lives in terms of engaging with other users, which can help with decision-making in multiple domains. In our work, we introduce a novel approach based on a hybrid model RoBERTa-TSGRU. This approach includes combining the Robustly Optimized BERT Pretraining Approach (RoBERTa) together with the Two-State Gated Recurrent Unit (TSGRU) network. In this proposed framework, RoBERTa is utilized for generating quality embeddings, while VADER (Valence Aware Dictionary and sEntiment Reasoner) assigns sentiment scores. Meanwhile, Word2Vec builds embedding vectors; furthermore, a TSGRU neural architecture enhanced by RoBERTa accounts for sequential and contextual embeddings. Moreover, in order to make the model effective in practice, this study converted RoBERTa-TSGRU-VADER to ONNX format and optimized it with OpenVINO in FP32 and FP16 modes, and then optimized further to INT8 with Neural Network Compression Framework (NNCF). The proposed model was tested on two popular datasets, IMDb movie reviews and Sentiment140. Performance-wise, the proposed RoBERTa-TSGRU-VADER model was compared to several state-of-the-art models, including BERT, RoBERTa-base, RoBERTa-GRU, RoBERTa-BiGRU, and RoBERTa-BiLSTM. Experimental results demonstrated that the proposed hybrid model showed better performance and obtained classification accuracies of 95.91% and 91.78% on IMDb and Sentiment140 datasets, respectively. Accordingly, the proposed model attained F1-scores of 96.02% and 91.15% on the same datasets, illustrating its efficiency in enhancing sentiment analysis performance.
